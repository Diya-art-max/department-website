About the Department

The Department of Life Sciences provides students with knowledge of different areas of biological science through an interdisciplinary approach.

The Life Sciences programme integrates Botany, Zoology and Chemistry. It encourages scientific thinking, practical learning and research skills.

Faculty and Academic Disciplines

Botany

Study of plants, their structure, diversity, physiology and environment.

Zoology

Study of animals, their behaviour, structure and biological functions.

Chemistry

Study of chemical substances and reactions involved in biological systems.

Courses Offered

The Life Sciences programme includes subjects from different scientific disciplines.
